## Adaptations

- `roof_cement_tile`: concrete colour and roughness from Concrete Wall 006, corrugation normal/AO from Corrugated Iron; metalness is zero. This is a composite approximating grey Thai fibre-cement roofing, not a scan of Thai double-wave tiles. A later roof model should build its corrugated silhouette in geometry.
- Plaster and cement coats neutralise the scan colour and retain its grain in the shader, so white plaster and seeded shophouse pastels read as paint rather than brown raw scan colour. Original asset files are unchanged.
- Road paint uses only plaster micro-normal/roughness/AO; the colour stays white or yellow. Curb red/white bands are generated in code over that surface.
- Glass uses the Surface Imperfections 001 water-smudge mask as roughness and its normal at low strength, with physical transmission; no opaque colour map.
- Water uses the same CC0 roughness mask, a generated periodic ripple normal, and Thai river/flood tints. This is a water shader material, not a photograph of water.
- Thai signs use generated canvas colour/emission maps and plaster micro-normal/roughness/AO. Lit windows share this neutral micro-surface.
- Leaf001 provides opacity for double-sided tropical leaf cards. Leaves vary by model tint, not by copying the material.
- Fabric Canvas uses the neutral beige Cotton Jersey scan as a coarse cloth base; its knit is an approximation of woven awning fabric. Its original provider-labelled 1K maps are 1024 × 1025, preserving the measured scan aspect; the other bundled surface maps have a 1024-pixel longest edge.
