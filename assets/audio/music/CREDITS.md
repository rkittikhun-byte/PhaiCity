# Music credits

Edited by hand. Every file listed in `tracks.json` needs a row here (a test checks this).
Rules for music, including what is forbidden, are in `docs/audio/AUDIO_PLAN.md` section 4;
the brief, AI prompts and file spec are in `docs/audio/SOUND_LIST.md` sections 6 and 9–12.

Unlike the code, music is not covered by the GPL. Each track keeps the license in its row:
CC0 tracks are public domain; commissioned tracks are used under the contract with their musician,
and their terms go in `assets/audio/LICENSE.md` before the first one is added.

The tracks below are temporary CC0 recordings from Freesound (the high-quality OGG preview of each),
used until made-for-PhaiCity music arrives. Credit is not required for CC0, but we list them with thanks.
